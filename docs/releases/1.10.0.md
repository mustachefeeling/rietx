## Upgrading

Code written against 1.9.0 runs unaltered, unless it imports one of the six indexing colour constants named below.

### Code to check

- `capabilities().schema_version` is `0.57`, where 1.9.0's was `0.55`.
  0.56 adds `RefinementResult.parametric_form`, and a result carrying it does not open in 1.9.0.
  0.57 adds `Phase.texture` and `RefinementResult.texture`, and a model or result carrying either does not open in 1.9.0.
- A GSAS-II project's spherical-harmonics entry is now `Gsas2Hap.texture`, and its `Gsas2Hap.preferred_orientation` is `None` (WP-1960).
  In 1.9.0 that field held the entry's unused March-Dollase slots under the model name `"SH"`.
- `rietx.viz.indexing` no longer has the colour constants `OBS`, `CALC`, `BKG`, `DIFF`, `ABSENT` and `UNMATCHED` (WP-1972).
  Their values are the `index_obs`, `index_calc`, `index_bkg`, `index_diff`, `absent` and `unmatched` entries of `rietx.viz.plots.PALETTES["light"]`. `CANDIDATE_COLORS` stays.

### Numbers that move

- A joint fit with per-histogram phase paths now holds a pattern's own copy of a phase's cell where that pattern cannot see the phase (WP-1325).
  In 1.9.0 the cell stayed free in that pattern whenever another pattern still showed the phase, so it could wander with nothing to fit.
  A fit moves only where a phase is absent from some of its patterns and its cell is per-histogram, as under `SharingMap(per_histogram=["phases.*"])`.

## New

- A joint fit now tests each parametric declaration's form against its own data (WP-1325, schema 0.56).
  `RefinementResult.parametric_form` holds one `ParametricFormTest` per declaration: how much the fit would improve with the target free in each pattern, over the noise level, against the 95 % point for its degrees of freedom.
  It is a score test at the answer and needs no refit; on a 33-pattern real ramp it cost 0.7-0.8 s and gave a full refit's verdict on all five laws tried.
  `str(result)` prints the verdict after each declaration's coefficients, and `PARAMETRIC_FORM_REJECTED` (warning) names the coefficients of a rejected law, whose esds assume the form.
  The test sees a law only where a pattern measures its target, so a law carried through patterns that cannot see a phase is not checked there.
- A notebook shows the pattern, fit, series and structure figures in the frontend's light or dark theme, and follows a theme switch without a rerun (WP-1970).
  A figure drawn light carries a dark copy as HTML beside its PNG, and what you add to the returned figure appears in both.
  JupyterLab, Notebook 7 and JupyterLite switch it; a frontend that strips the switch, such as GitHub's viewer, shows the light figure.
  A saved notebook stores about three times the image bytes per figure. `SeriesResult.plot` now takes `style="dark"` too.
- The indexing figures take `style="dark"` and follow a notebook's theme, as the pattern figures do (WP-1972).
  `plot_peak_list`, `plot_candidates`, `plot_validation` and `plot_indexing` each take it, and the light figures keep their colours.
- A phase can carry a spherical-harmonics texture, the preferred-orientation correction for a texture with no single axis (WP-1960, schema 0.57).
  `Phase.texture` declares an even order, and its coefficients refine from zero with exact analytic columns.
  The default driver keeps the pole density non-negative at every reflection, and `TEXTURE_NOT_POSITIVE` reports where it goes negative between them.
  `RefinementResult.texture` gives each textured phase's texture index J and the two minima of P.
  The `mccusker_structural` plan frees the coefficients beside March-Dollase's, and a phase declares one of the two.
  On the IUCr round-robin sample 2, the platy brucite's worst weighed-fraction error was 2.71 wt % at order 6, against 2.80 under March-Dollase.
- `to_structure` carries a GSAS-II project's spherical-harmonics texture, converted to this package's convention (WP-1960).
  Under `bragg_brentano` the carried texture gives GSAS-II's own multipliers to its single precision.
  `GSAS2_GPX_TEXTURE_NOT_CARRIED` says when it cannot: a Debye-Scherrer histogram, histograms that disagree, or terms GSAS-II 5.8.4 itself evaluates wrongly.

## Changed

- A figure a plot returns keeps its notebook size after a `set_xlim` or `set_ylim` of your own (WP-1971).
  In 1.9.0 the gutter labels and the statistics line were placed in data coordinates, and a zoom to a tenth of the x range made the notebook image 7.7 times wider.
  They now stay beside the axes, and a label your zoom leaves outside the axes is hidden.
- A figure a plot returns pickles, which raised `AttributeError` in 1.9.0 (WP-1971).
