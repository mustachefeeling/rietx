## New

- `result.plot()` names the radiation on its 2θ axis, as `data.plot()` does, from the new `RefinementResult.radiation` record (WP-2042).
  A joint fit records each pattern's own, and a result saved earlier draws the bare axis.
- `rietx.indexing.index_residue` indexes what one cell leaves of a pattern, as a second `IndexingResult` (WP-1969, #933).
  It fits the first result's top cell (or `candidate=`) by the Le Bail validation protocol, subtracts its peaks, picks the remainder at that fit's widths, flags a pick standing on more of the subtracted profile than its own height (`RESIDUE_MISFIT_RATIO`) as `residue_misfit`, and indexes the list; `INDEX_RESIDUE_PASS` names the cell and the flagged picks.
  A flagged pick stays a usable line, so the second result is graded with it counted as unindexed, and it never drives the search.
  A cell whose fit needs widths outside three times the list's own leaves no residue: nothing is picked, and the message says why (on #933's reproducer, whose first cell on `main` is a wrong cubic I one, that takes under a second where picking at its 2° widths took minutes).
  A list handed in as positions carries an assumed width, so the width test is skipped for it and the fit is not seeded from it.
  On synthetic Si with MgO at 0.05, 0.1 or 0.3 of its scale the second result holds MgO's cell second, behind the √2·a₀ body-centred cell that the flagged lines let into the first twenty; with NaCl at 0.1 it holds the cell first. All grade `low`. On SRM 660c and on synthetic Si alone no search is driven and it abstains. It is opt-in: `index_pattern` is unchanged, and the first result is never modified.
