## Changed

- `Refinement.report()`, `reflection_table()`, `predict()` with no grid and `write_cif()` read the answer saved with the result (WP-1421).
  A later fit that is cancelled keeps its completed stages, and these no longer mix its values into the previous result.
- `FROZEN_COMPILE_STALE` no longer says the fit is unbiased, and says how to re-solve (WP-1421).
  A joint fit can now carry it, naming the pattern whose gap is largest.
- A site within 5e-4 (fractional, per coordinate) of a special position is counted once, on the special position they collapse to, however its phase was built (#1007).
  In 1.9.0 only `Structure.from_cif` merged such a site (`CIF_SITE_TWINS_MERGED`, #765). A project file, an edited document or a structure built in code kept it general, so PbSO₄'s S2 moved 1e-4 off the Pnma mirror put 8 S in a cell of 4, and nothing said so.
  One gate at every boundary, readers included: a site is merged only where the occupancy at its position, summed over the atoms sharing it and each × (images before / after), is above 1, since the site as stored then puts more than one atom at one place. The project readers (`to_structure`), `RefinementTree.load`, the `Refinement` verbs that take a structure in (construction, `edit`, `set_values`, `checkout`), the CIF reader and `phase_zmv` all read it. A merge says `SITE_TWINS_MERGED` (warning; `CIF_SITE_TWINS_MERGED` on a CIF read); where the product is 1 or below the numbers read as one atom off its position or as a split pair (#1088), so the site is left as stored and `SITE_TWINS_KEPT` (warning) gives both readings. **Files that now open with the site as stored:** a project file or CIF whose site, at occupancy ≤ ½ on a mirror (or the matching fraction elsewhere), sits within 5e-4 of the position and was merged by 1.9.0's CIF reader. A merged site's coordinates the position fixes lose `vary`, and its anisotropic tensor is projected onto the allowed ones. `Phase` validation stays strict.
  ZMV, every weight fraction and the forward model move on such a phase; a split site further than that from every special position does not. The reach is 5e-4 per coordinate, where 1.9.0's CIF merge used 0.02 Å between images, so a CIF stating `y = 0.251` on a mirror site is no longer merged (at its own precision it excludes ¼). Holds, user ties and free paths on coordinate DOFs follow a site's DOF renumbering on every route that moves it, or are dropped and named (`SITE_TWINS_MERGED`, or `SITE_DOFS_RENUMBERED` where nothing merged).
