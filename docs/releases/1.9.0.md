# rietx 1.9.0

> **Staging file, not yet a release.** Opened 2026-10-12, the day 1.8.0 was
> cut, so that a break or a user-facing addition is recorded the day it lands
> (ROADMAP protocol rule 6). There is no `1.9.0` on PyPI and no `v1.9.0` tag
> until `RELEASING.md` is walked. The cut writes the framing, and checks this
> file against the tag range `v1.8.0..main`. Stage each note under the
> heading below that fits it, the layout 1.8.0 shipped in.

## Upgrading

Code written against 1.8.0 runs unaltered, with the exceptions in this list.

### Code to check

- `PatternData.two_theta` is `None` for a time-of-flight pattern, which carries `PatternData.tof` in µs instead (WP-1927, schema 0.53).
  Code that reads `two_theta` as always present should ask `PatternData.axis` first (`"two_theta"` or `"tof"`).
  A constant-wavelength pattern is unchanged, and a stored document loads as before.
  Every entry that needs a 2θ axis refuses a time-of-flight pattern by name.
- A GSAS file holding several banks is refused until `bank=` names one (WP-1927).
  1.8.0 read the first bank.
  The refusal names the banks the file holds, and `rietx.io.formats.gsas.gsas_banks` lists them.
  A single-bank file reads as before.
  A project that recorded no bank reopens on the file's first bank, and its stored fingerprint still checks that choice.
- A `.xy` file whose header states its x-axis unit is read in that unit (WP-1927).
  A stated time of flight goes to `PatternData.tof`.
  A stated d-spacing, Q, wavelength or energy is refused by name.
  1.8.0 read such a column as 2θ whenever its values lay between 0 and 180.
- `RefinementResult.for_histogram(h)` spells parameter paths as a single fit does (WP-1341).
  Its `parameters` keep histogram h's rows without their `hist.<h>.` prefix, and drop every other histogram's rows.
  In 1.8.0 the view carried every row of the joint result, with each histogram's own rows still scoped.
- `capabilities().schema_version` is `0.54`, where 1.8.0's was `0.49`.
  0.50 adds the tether and anti-bump restraints, and 0.51 adds `SeriesResult.relative_paths` and `.angle_paths`.
  0.52 adds `Phase.floating_origin` and `ParameterRow.origin_axis`, 0.53 the time-of-flight axis and source, and 0.54 the `"centroid"` origin.
  Every document 1.8.0 wrote opens unchanged.
  1.8.0 refuses a pattern, a structure, a history log or a series result that this release writes.
  Each carries one of the new keys, even where its value is null.

### Numbers that move

- Axial divergence no longer switches off when only one of `axial_sl` and `axial_hl` is zero (WP-1953).
  One ratio at zero now gives the limit of a vanishing one, a uniform smear, and only both at zero give the symmetric peak.
  A stage that refines one ratio while the other is held at zero now refines a real asymmetry, where before it changed nothing.
  On the QPA round-robin pattern cpd-1a that stage takes S/L from 0 to 0.041, Rwp falls from 17.13 % to 16.68 %, and no weight fraction moves by more than 0.04 wt %.
  A fit freeing both ratios could land one on zero under LM, meet the jump there, and crawl to its evaluation cap beside it.
  On the report-loop test that failed on Windows in 1.8.0, LM and TRF now end at the same χ²_red, 51.29977, with one ratio at 0.0413 and the other on zero.
  That test now passes on Windows, Linux and macOS.
- A Pawley fit with more than 128 columns now converges under LM (WP-1953).
  Above 128 columns each step is now a projected conjugate-gradient step, which always lowers the model, where the old approximate step often raised it.
  On the GSAS-II fluorapatite tutorial pattern, 343 columns, every stage converges at Rwp 7.824 %, where 1.8.0 stopped the `profile` stage at its cap at 8.036 %.
  TRF reaches 7.838 % there.
  On the 11-BM NAC pattern, 1 499 columns, every stage converges at Rwp 12.62 %, where 1.8.0 stopped at its cap at 15.34 % and TRF reaches 13.48 %.
- Parameters on an exactly flat direction no longer report an esd of 0 (WP-1930).
  The covariance now inverts each kept eigenvalue of the normal matrix by its magnitude.
  A flat direction comes back at a rounding-level eigenvalue of either sign, and a negative one used to give every parameter on it a negative variance, read as an esd of 0 and a correlation of 0.
  Those parameters now report the large esd and the ρ = ±1 a positive one always gave, and `HIGH_CORRELATION` names them.
  On the LaB₆ + cBN fit with six Lorentzian widths free, 1.8.0 raised no `HIGH_CORRELATION` on Linux.
  On macOS a 1e-14 change to one start value reproduced that, with six widths at ± 0.
  Now, over ten such starts, the widths read ± 8.9 to ± 3420 at nine, and at the unchanged start, where both flat directions fall under the cut, ± 5e-5 to ± 3e-3 with `COVARIANCE_DIRECTION_DISCARDED` naming all six.
  A fit whose normal matrix has no negative eigenvalue above the cut is unchanged to the bit.
- Under LM, a stage no longer crawls beside a parameter whose column vanishes, such as a moment angle at a direction the symmetry fixes (WP-1953).
  Each column now keeps the largest scale it has had in the stage.
  A column that is small from the stage's start has its curvature measured once λ climbs, since Gauss-Newton cannot see it.
  Such stages used to stop on `converged` far from a minimum, or run out their evaluations.
  A one-site moment fit started at 3e-6 μ_B now reaches χ²_red 2e-18, where 1.8.0 reported `converged` at 35.5.
  With every moment DOF freed in one stage, the two-site Pnma set now reaches 3.498 and 0.700 μ_B, where 1.8.0's best was the swapped minimum.
  `profile_interval` on an azimuth beside such a direction now gives ±0.0357 rad, where 1.8.0's refits mostly stopped at their start and gave ±0.0287 rad.
- A reflection tick now sits where the calculated curve draws its peak (WP-1908).
  `RefinementResult.ticks` added the zero shift and nothing else.
  A fit with a refined sample displacement, transparency or capillary offset therefore drew every tick off its peak.
  On a synthetic 0.5 mm displacement on a 217.5 mm circle the ticks sat 0.14° to 0.26° low, and every tick now lies within 0.01° of its peak.
  The joint runner's ticks, the live viewer's ticks and the lattice positions of the satellite report take the same correction.
  No fitted value, Rwp or weight fraction moves.
  The report does, because it reads the ticks.
  On twelve QPA round-robin fits (cpd-1a to 1h, cpd-1c over two ranges, cpd-2 and cpd-4), the unmatched observed peaks changed on eight, in both directions.
  The suggested actions on cpd-1c, cpd-1f, cpd-1h and cpd-2 fell from eight, seven, seven and nine entries to one, one, none and one.
- A phase that a plan has called unseen stays held at the next stage's start (WP-1523).
  Each stage now reads the previous stage's answer beside its quick screen.
  Before, a phase whose scale had been fitted to noise was freed at every stage start, chased the noise, collapsed and was solved a second time.
  On a synthetic blank frame (issue #481) under the 11-stage structural plan that took 18 solves and 233 iterations, and it now takes 11 and 59.
  Of 64 fits of weak real phases, one went from refined to held, at 2.46σ.
  `run_stage`, `cherry_pick` and the GUI's stage-by-stage path still read the screen alone.
- Whether a phase is seen is now judged with the other phases' scales held (WP-1523).
  A phase modelled beside a subgroup of itself has a scale that trades one for one with the subgroup's.
  Judged with the partner's scale free, both phases read under 3σ, and the hold took whichever one the start left weaker.
  On issue #219's cubic Cu with an absent subgroup, that was the only real phase in 9 of 15 split starts.
  Now a phase that another can stand in for is not called absent, and `HIGH_CORRELATION` on the two scales reports the ambiguity.
  A phase fitted to noise still reads under 3σ.
- An indexing candidate whose ambiguity sweep refuses now counts as unasked, as one the clock stopped does (WP-1924).
  A sweep refuses when the candidate's own lines cannot be predicted.
  1.8.0 recorded such a candidate as checked with no partners, so the gate could still grade it `high`.
- `determine_extinction_symbol` now refits every declared `HumpComponent` in each class fit, beside the background, its FWHM floored at `HUMP_MIN_WIDTH_MULT` instrument widths ([#932](https://github.com/yue-here/rietx/pull/932) by @mustachefeeling, issue #931).
  Before, a declared container halo was held at the pre-fit's values, which a Le Bail line at a forbidden position under the halo had biased, and the class forbidding that line was refuted.
  On a synthetic pyrochlore with a 1° halo on the forbidden (200), `F d - -` is now returned on 3 of 3 noise seeds, where 1.8.0 refuted it on all three; a real line at (200) still refutes it.
  A screen with no declared hump is unchanged.

### Files that change

## New

- `TetherRestraint` and `AntiBumpRestraint` join the `Restraint` union (schema 0.50).
  A tether holds two atoms within a maximum distance, and an anti-bump keeps them beyond a minimum.
  Each row is zero on its flat side, as TOPAS's `Distance_Restrain_Keep_Within` and `Keep_Out` are.
  `rietx.model.restraints.anti_bump_restraints(phase, min_distance)` builds the anti-bump rows for every close pair and image ([#888](https://github.com/yue-here/rietx/pull/888) by @mustachefeeling, WP-1809).
  `RestraintRow.kind` gains `"tether"` and `"anti_bump"`.
- Under `direction="both"`, `SequentialRefinement.forward_` holds the forward chain's `SeriesResult` from before the backward walk starts ([#903](https://github.com/yue-here/rietx/pull/903) by @mustachefeeling, WP-1317, issue #218).
  A run can be judged at the halfway point.
- `SeriesResult.moved_paths(n, min_sigma)` gives the paths that moved most against their own esds, ready for `SeriesResult.plot`.
  It reads the new `SeriesResult.relative_paths` and `.angle_paths` (schema 0.51), which a run now records.
- `Phase.floating_origin = "hold"` (schema 0.52): the parameter table holds, for each direction a polar group's origin floats in, one coordinate DOF a shift along it moves, so a fit freeing every coordinate cannot slide the structure (WP-1912, #727).
  It is off by default.
  `Refinement.hold_floating_origin()` now sets the field rather than declaring a hold of its own, so a series pattern, a joint histogram and a history replay hold the same atom.
  `unhold` on the held DOF sets the field back to `None`, and `ParameterRow.origin_axis` names the direction a held row fixes.
  A fit reports `ORIGIN_FIXED_ON_POLAR_AXIS` once, in place of the `HOLD_BLOCKED_PLAN` it used to raise for that DOF, and a joint fit now reports it too.
  `ORIGIN_NOT_FIXED` (warning) says every DOF the shift moves was tied or held while a stage freed what walks the direction, and `hold_floating_origin()` then leaves the phase undeclared.
  `magnetic_supercell` copies the field to the child phase.
- `Phase.floating_origin = "centroid"` (schema 0.54): instead of holding an atom, each stage that can slide a polar phase restrains the weighted centroid of its sites, with the weights Flack & Schwarzenbach (1988) derive from the data (WP-1912, #727).
  Every coordinate along the axis keeps an esd; on synthetic zincite the structure and Rwp equal the hold's, and each z carries the difference's esd over √2.
  The restraint is a residual row block of its own (`origin_restraint`), which a stage's restraint weight never scales, and `ORIGIN_FIXED_ON_POLAR_AXIS` names the sites with their weights.
  A joint fit refuses it by name. The default stays `None`.

### Joint fits

- `MultiHistogramRefinement.fit` and `refine_multi` take `events=`, `cancel=`, `telemetry=`, `label=` and `progress=`, as `Refinement.fit` does, and a joint fit records one run directory (WP-1341, #252).
  Each event kind is emitted once per fit, stage or evaluation, and a fact about one histogram rides in a `histograms` row stamped with its `histogram_index`.
  The event schema version does not move.
- A joint result's `Statistics.max_shift_over_esd` is measured, over the joint parameter vector, where it was `None`.
- `MultiHistogramRefinement.report(h)` builds one histogram's `FitReport` at Layers 0 to 2.
  The joint result now carries each histogram's background screen in `Identifiability.background_absorption`, under `hist.<h>.` keys, where `identifiability` was `None`.
- A joint plan naming a locked or symmetry-tied literal phase path now reports `STAGE_PATH_NOT_FREE`, as a single fit has since #789 (#803).

### TOPAS files

- `write_topas_inp(..., p1_expand=True)` beside `free=`, `scale=`, `instrument=` or `pattern=` writes every copy of a site as an equation over the parameter it copies, so the P 1 file refines as the model does ([#912](https://github.com/yue-here/rietx/pull/912) by @mustachefeeling, WP-1911, issue #721).
  In 1.8.0 that combination was refused, and a bare `p1_expand=True` file, which still holds every value, is unchanged.
  `p1_expand="auto"` restates only a phase TOPAS cannot name (a k ≠ 0 supercell, an untabulated family group, a non-standard setting) and reports `TOPAS_PHASE_RESTATED_IN_P1`.
- With `instrument=`, a phase with a magnetic-only width is written as two `str`, the nuclear part and a `mag_only` magnetic part carrying the width, the form `read_topas_inp` merges back.
  1.8.0 refused it by name; a file without `instrument=` still names it, `TOPAS_FIELD_NOT_WRITTEN`.
  The split is refused when extinction is non-zero or free, because rietx applies one Sabine factor to both parts and TOPAS cannot.

### Time-of-flight readers

- A neutron time-of-flight bank can be read and inspected, not yet refined (WP-1927, [#911](https://github.com/yue-here/rietx/pull/911) by @mustachefeeling, schema 0.53).
  `read_pattern` opens a GSAS bank with a declared flight-time axis, and a two-column `.xy` file that states a flight time, onto `PatternData.tof`.
  A file of several banks is read one bank at a time with `bank=`, and `gsas_banks` lists them.
  `PatternData.x()` returns whichever axis is set, and `tt()` and `tof_us()` each refuse the other axis.
  `PatternData.axis_unit` names the unit.
- `Instrument.source` has a third arm, `TOFSource` (`kind="neutron_tof"`), holding DIFC, DIFA, TZERO, DIFB, the bank angle, the peak shape and the incident spectrum.
  Its `tof_from_d` and `d_from_tof` methods convert between flight time and d-spacing.
  `Instrument.tof_neutron_bank(difc, two_theta_bank_deg=...)` builds a bank's instrument by hand.
  The peak shape and spectrum types, `ProfileTOF` and `IncidentSpectrum`, are exported.
- Instrument readers: `read_gsas_tof_iparm` for GSAS-I `.iparm`, `read_gsas2_instprm` now handles a `Type: PNT` `.instprm`, and `rietx.io.legacy.read_lansce_iparm` reads the legacy LANSCE layout.
  Into a `diagnostics=` list they report the profile set read or declined (`GSAS_IPARM_PROFILE_READ`, `GSAS_IPARM_PROFILE_DECLINED`), the fields with nowhere to go (`GSAS_IPARM_FIELD_DROPPED`, `GSAS2_INSTPRM_FIELD_DROPPED`) and the legacy layout (`GSAS_IPARM_LEGACY_LAYOUT`).
- Nothing refines a bank in this build.
  `capabilities()` lists the new source, and the instrument writers refuse a bank by name.

## Changed

- A monoclinic search by the dichotomy engine holds 0.6-0.7 GB on the synthetic 224 Å³ case of issue #780, where 1.8.0 held 6.8 GB (WP-1924).
  1.8.0 copied the rows of every surviving grid cell at once and kept them until the search ended.
  Each grid cell's rows are now gathered when the search reaches the cell.
  A finished search returns the same candidates to the bit.
- Stop, and a whole-run ceiling, now take effect within one derivative cell of a candidate's ambiguity sweep (WP-1924, issue #895).
  In 1.8.0 they waited for the whole sweep.
  A sweep they stop leaves its candidate unasked.
- A figure shown in a notebook declares its width alone, where it declared its width and height (WP-1957).
  A frontend that narrows an image to its output column then scales the figure whole.
  With both declared, a column narrower than the figure squashed it.
  That is the likely cause of the squashed figures seen in Colab.
- A structure figure shown in a notebook is drawn at half its pixel width, as a fit figure is, and quantised to 256 colours.
  The fluorapatite view down c is 74 kB, where it was 185 kB.
  A transparent figure, a file written with `path=`, and an install without pillow keep full colour.
- Tutorial notebook 06, "A Rietveld refinement on your own data", is a template.
  It runs on the fluorapatite example until you change its marked cells to point at your own files.
  Notebook 02 now shows its first fit as soon as it ends, and draws the refined structure.
