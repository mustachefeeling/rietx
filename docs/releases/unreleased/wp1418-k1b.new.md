- **`search_kvector`: a k-search that ranks and does not pick** (WP-1418 K1b, issue #795).
  `rietx.indexing.ksearch.search_kvector(peaks, parent)` scores every k on a 1/64 grid
  against the unexplained peaks and returns a `KSearchResult` ranked by each row's adjusted
  p under `kgrid_bound` at `alpha=0.01` (flat weighting). Peaks within one local nuclear
  FWHM of a nuclear line are set aside and the same windows bound the chance level
  (`sorted_out`, `exclude`). `crowding` reports how strongly the peaks kept crowd the
  nuclear lines (a one-sided Kolmogorov-Smirnov p), and `K_SEARCH_CROWDED` warns below
  `alpha`; `K_SEARCH_AT_CHANCE`, `K_SEARCH_FEW_PEAKS` and `K_SEARCH_CUT` say when nothing
  clears, when no k could, and when the budget cut the search. `best_or_none()` returns
  `None` on every call, the reason on `withheld`: a k ranked from positions is confirmed by
  refinement, as an indexed cell is.
