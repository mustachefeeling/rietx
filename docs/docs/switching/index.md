# Switching

These pages are for users of TOPAS, GSAS-II and FullProf, and say what rietx does the same, what it does differently and how it was validated.

- [rietx at a glance](at-a-glance.md) lists the data rietx fits and each feature with what it was checked against.
- [Coming from TOPAS](topas.md), [Coming from GSAS-II](gsas2.md) and [Coming from FullProf](fullprof.md) give the differences in concepts, paired examples, and how to move a file across.
- [Validation against other codes](validation.md) gives each comparison with its protocol and its numbers.
- [Roadmap](roadmap.md) lists the milestones in progress and planned.
- [Names in other programs](../reference/foreign-names.md) maps each parameter to its name and unit in the four programs.
