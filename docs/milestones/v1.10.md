## How v1.10 is getting here

### Parametric laws tested against their data (1325, in flight)

A parametric fit's coefficient esds assume the declared law. Since
2026-10-14 every parametric joint fit also reports whether its data rejects
that law, by a score test at the answer (`RefinementResult.parametric_form`,
`PARAMETRIC_FORM_REJECTED`). On the ZrMo₂O₈ ramp it matched a full refit on
all five laws tried, for one Jacobian. It is blind where no pattern sees the
target, which [1963](../wp/1963-a-kernel-over-the-pattern-coordinates.md)
was filed to address. The same WP's #970 fixed the joint hold for a
per-histogram cell its own pattern cannot see. Both are staged in the notes.

The maintainer's rule for laws is that the output shows which values came
from one. Since the seventh session each joint row names the law that set it
(`RefinedParameter.laws`, schema 0.57), and `PARAMETRIC_TARGET_UNSEEN` names
the patterns where a law sets a target its pattern cannot see. The verdict
line now gives its p-value and counts the patterns tested against the subset.
