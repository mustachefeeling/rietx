### The skill's evaluation strategy, verified on a pilot (1904, closed 2026-10-04)

Off the milestone. [1904](../wp/1904-the-skill-evaluation-strategy.md)
answered how the agent skill is to be measured, rewritten and kept in sync,
and tried the answer before filing it. The harness is Claude Code's own
`claude plugin eval`, run over a plugin built from the skill tree: two cases
from the repository's own episodes scored with free trace graders and a
rubric, both arms, for under a dollar a case. An audit put the body at 8 700
tokens with rules at 43 % of its bytes and 8 kB restated in the references;
a prototype cut it to 5 300 tokens and passed every gate. The pilot refuted
two assumptions on the way: the existing judging episode is a ceiling on
Sonnet, and a refinement with the skill does more work than one without it,
which decided half the cells by wall clock rather than by the answer. The
work is 1905 (the suite), 1906 (the rewrite) and 1907 (constants, formulas
and a changed-surface check at review).

### Extinction's unit and its typical size (1451, closed 2026-10-04)

Off the milestone. [1451](../wp/1451-the-extinction-a-powder-has.md) began
as issue #419: rietx called its Sabine powder correction secondary extinction,
and the paper says a powder has only primary. A contributor's PR #533 renamed
it everywhere a user meets the name, and both Sabine papers were read to give
each of the six Laue coefficients a stated source; the last two are GSAS-II's,
and its sixth is 0.5 % off the exact series term, kept for the cross-code
golden. The review found the parameter had no declared unit and a help range
a thousand times too small to dim anything. It is now declared as a squared
block size in µm², and the range comes from five structures, where a strong
reflection loses 1 % at 0.1-8 µm². No fit's numbers moved.
