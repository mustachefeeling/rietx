### The skill's evaluation strategy, verified on a pilot (1904, closed 2026-10-04)

Off the milestone. [1904](../wp/1904-the-skill-evaluation-strategy.md)
answered how the agent skill is to be measured, rewritten and kept in sync,
and tried the answer before filing it. The harness is Claude Code's own
`claude plugin eval`, run over a plugin built from the skill tree: two cases
from the repository's own episodes scored with free trace graders and a
rubric, both arms, for under a dollar a case. An audit put the body at 8 700
tokens with rules at 43 % of its bytes and 8 kB restated in the references;
a prototype cut it to 5 300 tokens and passed every gate. The pilot refuted
two assumptions on the way: the existing judging episode is a ceiling on
Sonnet, and a refinement with the skill does more work than one without it,
which decided half the cells by wall clock rather than by the answer. The
work is 1905 (the suite), 1906 (the rewrite) and 1907 (constants, formulas
and a changed-surface check at review).

### A displacement parameter with no default bound (1534, closed 2026-10-04)

Off the milestone. [1534](../wp/1534-a-scale-and-a-b-the-range-cannot-separate.md)
began from issue #204, where Fe's B walked to −165 Å² along a scale–B ridge and
two staging orders gave 0.0 and 80.9 wt% at one Rwp. PR #673 had landed a hold:
a stage freezes B where the fitted range puts a phase at one d-spacing. This
session settled the question PR #663 had deferred. `Atom.biso` is now
unbounded, as in FullProf, TOPAS and GSAS-II. The three guideline papers ask a
code to warn, never to bound. On the IUCr round-robin mixtures the old 0–25 Å²
range moved no fraction by more than 0.03 wt% over a full scan, and helped by
about 1 wt% RMS on 20–40°, where every case was flagged. Measuring it found a
case the hold missed: fluorite's two sites against two reflections walked to
+99 and −111 Å² and returned 0.0 wt% against 1.36 weighed. The hold now asks
the phase's whole displacement block against its scale, in single and joint
fits alike, and returns 1.32. The break is staged in the
[1.7 notes](../releases/1.7.0.md). Two defects it found are filed: the ticks
miss the displacement shift (1908), and a phase with no reflection in range can
walk its scale (1523's Inherited).
