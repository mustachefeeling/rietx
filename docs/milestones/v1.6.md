### A phase at zero keeps its fraction esds (1463, closed 2026-09-28)

Off the milestone and staged in the same notes.
[1463](../wp/1463-a-phase-at-zero-withholds-every-esd.md) came from a transcript
review. On 16 of 48 patterns of a real series every weight-fraction esd was
`None`, no finding said why, and the agent propagated the scale esds by hand.
The cause was arithmetic. An absent phase's scale column is as small as the
scale, and below about 1e-163 its internal variance overflowed, while its
physical esd was an ordinary 7.66e-9. The WP had named the d² underflow,
which arrives seven orders later. A column outside [2⁻⁴⁰⁰, 2⁴⁰⁰] is now
rescaled by an exact power of two before anything squares it, so every
answer down to about 1e-300 equals the 1e-135 one, and an ordinary fit keeps
every bit. At exactly 0.0 the parameterisation has lost the direction, so the
esds stay `None` and `QPA_ESD_UNAVAILABLE` names the phase. A softplus floor
reads `at_bound=None`. The joint fit had its own QPA builder, and it reported
1.0 ± 0.0 in the same state. A solver floor was measured and set aside: it
moved an ordinary fit and drove the absent scale deeper.

### A trace fraction is profiled, not trusted (1320, closed 2026-09-27)

### A checkout stamps what it runs (1456, closed 2026-09-27)

The ninth silent-answer fix, from the same reviews of agent sessions. Two
sessions ran 1.6.0.dev0 code from a checkout whose editable install predated
every version bump since 1.4.0. Their results, their reports and 887 run records all said

stamped exactly as before. The run records turned out to have their own
metadata lookup, which the fix to the main stamp would never have reached. A
meta-test now holds one reader.

### A direction the covariance discards (1535, closed 2026-10-02)

WP-1534 found that `pinv` hands a discarded direction zero variance, so two
exactly collinear columns came back with a tight esd. The pair case already had
`FLAT_DIRECTION`. 1535 asked how often a *combination* is discarded and nothing
shows it. On the acceptance fixtures it is common: 183 of 1 899 whole-pattern
solves (9.6 %, numpy, Darwin), most within a factor of ten of the cut, spread
over four to eight columns, in the QPA, SRM 676a and brucite fixtures. The fast
selection has 24 of 3 100 (0.8 %), all Pawley overlaps or constructed pairs. A
one-site occupancy against its scale confirmed as an exact degeneracy (λ 3.1e-16
against a cut of 2.0e-15), and it already carried `FLAT_DIRECTION`.

The decision was a finding, `COVARIANCE_DIRECTION_DISCARDED`, and the esds stay.
Marking them absent would have blanked the headline fixtures on a threshold set
by rounding. A parameter is named when the discarded subspace adds at least as
much variance as the solve reports, which makes its esd at least √2 short. That
bar is the cut itself, summed over the subspace because two discarded directions
are only defined up to a rotation. On cpd-1a it names the sample-broadening block
(size, strain and the instrument widths), which is the trade-off a pair test
cannot see. The finding is retaken on the answer-producing stage and skipped for
Pawley solves, whose cut matrix `outcome.jac` does not hold.
