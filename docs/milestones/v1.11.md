## How v1.11 is getting here

### 1.10.0 cut and published (2043, closed 2026-10-24)

The fourth weekly cut came ten days after 1.9.0, three days late, because the
Windows gate had been red since 2026-10-20 and the cut found two defects
worth holding for. Chasing the macOS split in the QPA guide's esd led to
GSAS-II's Sabine extinction factor, which every release had copied. Its 3.6 %
step at x = 1 made an esd depend on whether the last finite difference
straddled the step. The maintainer had the continuous function land first
(WP-2044). The VALIDATION re-measure found the hostile round-robin series
spending a very long time in the closing stage every plan now ends with. The
maintainer chose to ship with that stated as a limit, with a measured
workaround, and to fix it in WP-2045. The cut carried WP-2002's closing stage
and removed limits, the purposes WP-2037 prints diagnostics by, and the new
manual, and it completed no milestone. On release day the Windows gate went
red once more, on a test-side path separator in the eval harness, and was
fixed before the tag (#1151). On the release commit the Windows suite passed
every test while its job ran six seconds past its 45-minute limit, and the
maintainer took the suite's result as the gate. The `full` nightly finished on
Linux for the first time since the closing stage landed, with three slow rows
red (WP-2036). 1.10 is the first release to carry its own manual, now
`stable` at `/docs/`. The measured ship block is the
[v1.10 record](v1.10.md)'s appendix.
