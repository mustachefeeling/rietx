# v1.9 — the next release

**Accumulating since 2026-10-12** (version → `1.9.0.dev0`), the day 1.8.0 was
cut. This is the record of release 1.9: the narrative of everything landing on
`main` before the cut, and the measured ship block after it (WP-1540). A
milestone's own scope and acceptance live in its named record
([magnetic.md](magnetic.md) for the one open now).

## Milestones this release completes

None yet. A milestone joins this list when its own record's acceptance is
met, and the cut lists the ones it ships.

## How v1.9 is getting here

### A TOPAS P 1 file that refines as the model does (1911, in flight)

@mustachefeeling's #912 writes each copy of a site in a P 1 restatement as an
equation over the parameter it copies. Until now every copy was its own held
parameter, so TOPAS could check the file but not refine it. `p1_expand="auto"`
restates only a phase whose magnetic group TOPAS cannot name, and says so. A
whole input now writes a magnetic-only width as a second `str`. It refuses the
split when extinction is non-zero or free, since rietx applies one Sabine factor to both
parts and TOPAS cannot. The schema did not move.

### The forward pass at the halfway point (1317, in flight)

@mustachefeeling's #903 publishes a `direction="both"` series' forward chain
as `SequentialRefinement.forward_` before the backward walk starts. The
backward walk roughly doubles the wall clock, so #218 asked for a chain in the
wrong basin to be caught at the halfway point. `SeriesResult.moved_paths`
ranks the paths that moved most against their own esds and leaves out the
ones with no esd. The scrubber this WP is for is unstarted. The schema moved to
0.51.

### One-sided distance restraints (1809, in flight)

Two restraint kinds hold a distance on one side only, built by
@mustachefeeling in #888. A tether keeps two atoms within a maximum distance,
and an anti-bump keeps them beyond a minimum. Each row is zero on its flat
side, as TOPAS's `Distance_Restrain_Keep_Within` and `Keep_Out` are. The caller
builds the anti-bump rows with `anti_bump_restraints`. It lists every pair
and image inside the cut, and it gives no row to a species pair with no
stated minimum. The record had the engine freeze that list at plan compile. The
review kept the caller's form, because explicit rows hold the row count fixed
across stages and survive a history checkout. Planarity and a plan-end
diagnostic for pairs that drift inside the cut are still open. The schema
moved to 0.50.

### A flat direction reads as undetermined on every platform (1930, closed 2026-10-13)

Issue #832 found the LaB₆ + cBN acceptance fit at χ²_red 12.48 on Linux and
9.69 on macOS, every assertion green. One Mac reproduced both answers when a
start value moved by 3e-14, so rounding chose the minimum, and the split
predated the #700 merge the bisect named. The fix holds the Gaussian triple,
which this histogram cannot determine. WP-1938 then refined every width in
its own units, so `profile.y` leaves its floor without a seed. The band
assertion is green in four Linux nightlies and on macOS arm64.

WP-1938 brought a second split. On the degenerate plan the six Lorentzian
widths span two flat directions, whose eigenvalues reach the covariance at
the rounding level with either sign. The pseudo-inverse inverted a negative
one with its sign, so the widths read ± 0 and `HIGH_CORRELATION` went
silent on Linux. The solve now inverts each kept eigenvalue by its
magnitude. A fit with no such eigenvalue is unchanged to the bit. The macOS
nightly's red dedup test was the axial pair's zero column at its floor,
which WP-1953's #918 fixed. The same sign inversion in indexing's
`equal_reduced` is fixed the same way, and 22 indexing units replay bit
for bit. On Linux the degenerate plan's flat directions fall under the cut,
so they are named by `COVARIANCE_DIRECTION_DISCARDED` rather than
`HIGH_CORRELATION`, and the acceptance suite accepts either. Moving the cut
to numpy's n·eps, or marking the columns those directions touch as
unmeasured, was measured and refused: both remove warnings from fits that
pass today.

### LM converges where it says it does (1953, closed 2026-10-13)

The default LM driver stopped early in three places, and two of them reported
`converged`. The first was the 1.8.0 Windows gate's known issue. The
axial-divergence profile jumped when one aperture reached zero, and LM's exact
step landed on that zero. The stage then ran out its budget wherever rounding
left it. One aperture at zero is now the physical limit, and `test_e8` ends at
one χ²_red on Windows, Linux and macOS. The second was a Pawley fit above 128
columns. Its approximate step was often wrong in sign, and projected conjugate
gradients replaced it. FAP.XRA and 11-BM NAC Pawley now converge every stage.
Each reaches one answer from five starts, at or below TRF's Rwp. The third was
a column that vanishes at its minimum, such as a moment angle at a direction
the symmetry fixes. LM now keeps each column's largest scale for the stage. It
also measures the curvature of a column Gauss-Newton cannot see. The two-site
moment set now finds the truth on every CI runner, arm64 Linux included.
Fits that free one aperture beside a zero one now move. QPA cpd-1a's Rwp went
from 0.17125 to 0.16676. Two review findings went to WP-1955.

### A tick sits where the model draws its peak (1908, closed 2026-10-13)

A result placed each reflection tick at its Bragg angle plus the zero shift.
The forward model also applies the sample displacement, the transparency and
the capillary offsets. A fit with a refined displacement therefore drew its
ticks off the peaks it had fitted. On a synthetic 0.5 mm displacement they sat
0.14° to 0.26° low, and on cpd-1c under the QPA plan 0.50° to 0.65° low. The
forward model now exposes its position as one method, and the result's
builder, the joint runner's, the live viewer's and the satellite report's
lattice all call it. Every tick of the synthetic case lies within 0.01° of its
peak. No fitted number moves. The report moves, because it reads the ticks: on
twelve QPA fits the suggested actions fell from as many as nine to one or
none, and the unmatched observed peaks changed on eight, in both directions.
The peaks that became unmatched on cpd-1c are flank lobes of strong zincite
lines, 0.15° to 0.19° from the tick. Whether Layer 0 should call such a lobe an
unindexed peak is open, and no work package owns it. The review found the
satellite report adding the zero alone and fixed it in the same pull request.

### A phase fitted to noise is held from either start (1523, closed 2026-10-13)

Issue #481's blank frame holds no phase, yet a fit to it reported LaB6's cell
with an esd or held it, depending on where the fit started. The support test
read one point of the phase's curve at 1σ, and a scale fitted to noise lifts
that point past it, because the fit also moves the cell to wherever the noise
peaks. A phase now counts as seen when its scale is 3σ from zero, its esd
taken against counting noise and marginal over every column that moves the
peaks. Indexing asks a line for the same 3σ. On 20 blank frames it calls one
seen, where the old test called nine. The quick screen became the integrated
norm, which bounds that test from above, and a plan's stage now reads the
previous answer beside it. Before that, a noise phase whose screen passed was
freed at every stage start and solved twice: on the 11-stage structural plan
one blank took 18 solves, and now takes 11. A phase at about 1/16 000 of a
strong one's scale is now mostly held, and its cell came back up to 1000 ppm
off when refined, so the hold loses no measurement.

The last task was a collinear pair: issue #219's cubic Cu, modelled with an
absent subgroup whose scale trades one-for-one with the parent's. Judged
with the partner's scale free to compensate, both phases read under 3σ, and
the hold took whichever the start left weaker. That was the only real phase
in 9 of 15 split starts. Each scale is now judged with the other phases'
scales held, so a phase another can stand in for is not called absent, and
`HIGH_CORRELATION` on the two scales reports the ambiguity. A phase fitted to
noise still reads low, because its inflation is its own cell and widths. A
probe over the hold, QPA and slow-tier fixtures (3409 readings) found no
other decision changed. The wrong-sign tetragonality the triage reported
comes from the degenerate start, not the hold. It returns with no hold at
all, and stays with WP-1339. The `trf` scale walk of a phase with no
reflection in range became WP-1957.
