# v1.3 — agents and programs

**Opened 2026-08-28** (version → `1.3.0.dev0`), the day v1.2 shipped. The
slot was queued the same day; free-standing peaks (1101-1103) shift to v1.4
and keep their numbers, the precedent v1.1 and v1.2 both set.

## Scope

The agent-facing surface refactored against two measured runs rather than
intuition — `../ROADMAP.md` § v1.3 states the evidence and the seven WPs, and
each WP file carries its own baseline. Nothing is restated here.

## How v1.3 is getting here — the rolling narrative

- **2026-08-28** — [1301](../wp/1301-hold-unsupported-phase.md) shipped: a
  phase the data cannot see is **held** for the stage rather than bounded, its
  scale left free so it can still appear, and `StageResult.held`/`.released`
  record what happened. The WP as filed covered only the phase that is
  invisible when the stage *starts*, which is not the case the in-situ ramp
  hit — `phase_support` measures the modelled contribution, so a phase seeded
  at scale 1e-4 is visible at stage start whatever the specimen holds, and the
  flat direction opens only as the solve drives that scale to nothing. So
  support is re-measured at the answer and moves both ways: a phase that
  appeared is released and the stage re-solved, one that collapsed is put back
  where the stage found it and held. One extra solve covers either, never a
  third, and the restore is licensed by the measurement that licensed the
  hold. Measured on the ramp's 13 sub-onset patterns through the agent's exact
  call: 2164 iterations and a cell 14.9 Å from truth on `main` unbounded, 1342
  and 0.163 Å with the bounds that agent added, 1669 and **no cell reported at
  all** here — a held parameter leaves `RefinementResult.parameters`, so a
  trajectory of a phase that appears part-way through starts at the onset.
  `SCHEMA_VERSION` 0.10 → 0.11. Three rules entered CLAUDE.md's flat-direction
  invariant, and the WP's log has the rest. Next: WP-1302.

- **2026-08-29** — [1306](../wp/1306-powderline-recipe.md) shipped: `rx.read_recipe`
  reads a PowderLine `GSASII_Rietveld` JSON recipe into a structure, an
  instrument, a pattern, a plan and its `fit_range`, and `rx.write_recipe_tables`
  writes a finished refinement back as its four tables — so this package is now
  an engine such a pipeline can dispatch to. That is the deliverable and it is
  not the finding. **The finding is what two reference engines do to a bar.**
  Each committed fixture is one pattern refined by GSAS-II *and* TOPAS with both
  outputs in the repository, and on the two-phase cathode their cubic cells
  differ by **2665 ppm** — nine times the ±300 ppm the FAP suite uses — so the
  WP's own acceptance ("within ±300 ppm of GSAS-II *and* TOPAS") was
  arithmetically impossible and was rewritten as the **envelope** they bracket,
  widened by ±300 ppm at each end. It is not a mystery either: upstream's own
  file records two SVD singularities and a 100 % `Mustrain;mx`/`;i` correlation
  on that recipe, GSAS-II returns a **negative** crystallite size for one phase
  and TOPAS returns 5×10⁸ µm. Where this package falls in that argument is the
  result — **11-93 ppm from TOPAS on all five free cell parameters, at Rwp
  7.333 % against TOPAS's 7.326 %**, while sitting the same 1004-2575 ppm from
  GSAS-II that TOPAS does — and it is asserted as its own row rather than folded
  into the gate. The rule carried up: **two references are an envelope, not a
  second tolerance**, and across engines the cells are comparable while the
  broadening coefficients are not.
  Every unit was **measured before the reader was written**, against the
  reference output rather than the format's prose: their peak list's
  `sigma_squared` reproduces `U tan²θ + V tanθ + W` to six decimals on all 49
  LaB6 reflections, the drawn FWHM of their own `y_calc` fixes the centidegree
  unit to 0.1-0.9 %, and the excess `gamma` is exactly GSAS-II's undocumented
  1 µm / 1000 microstrain fill for a null magnitude. Three conventions the
  format states nowhere came out of that: that fill, a **0.001 centideg floor**
  on gamma that clamps 26 of its 49 LaB6 reflections, and its keeping the first
  channel past the stated upper limit (3768 against this package's 3767). `Zero`
  is the one row **refused** rather than chosen — upstream converts it as
  centidegrees in one module and annotates it "degrees 2theta" in another, 100×
  apart, and no committed recipe carries a non-zero one to settle it. Three more
  refusals and eight `RECIPE_*` reports draw the same line by magnitude: a value
  at the model's identity is dropped and said, a live one contradicts and raises.
  Two things landed against the WP's letter and both were forced by measurement.
  The plan is **staged, not the recipe's single pass** — translating "N cycles of
  everything flagged" faithfully walked the monoclinic cell to a = 4231 Å from a
  cold start; staging here is cumulative, so the last stage *is* that single
  pass and only the route differs, which is the engine's business and not the
  recipe's. And a broadening half that `LG_eta` puts at exactly zero is **held
  rather than declared free**: softplus's slope is its own value there, so
  freeing it is WP-1076's dead column. Neither engine's project defaults were
  adopted, and neither its Chebyshev coefficients nor its phase scale carried
  across — both are normalised in that engine's units, and GSAS-II and TOPAS
  converge four orders apart on one specimen's scale. `capabilities().features`
  gains `powderline_recipe`; no schema moved, so `SCHEMA_VERSION` stays at 0.14.
  Next: WP-1307, the last of the block.
