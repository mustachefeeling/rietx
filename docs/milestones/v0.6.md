## WP-0605 — batched peak loop: a measured no-go, and the cheap win instead

The WP's founding figure (2.4× from batching the per-peak loop) was a
fixed-work microbenchmark. On the real fits it inverts: the FCJ padded plane
is a **0.58× regression** (node-axis padding waste ~2.5×), while the
symmetric-row batch alone is **1.6× and exactly bit-equal** — evidence banked
for a future `vmap` series, not shipped. What shipped instead: an FCJ node
memo on exact input equality plus an `axial_derivs` skip in
`derivative_bases` — **1.23× on the SRM 660c protocol, bit-identical to the
last parameter bit**. The WP's own dirty-flag design measured ≈1.0× because
the staged plans free cumulatively, so no late stage is ever position-static.

Two profile facts worth keeping: `derivative_bases` costs ~2× the forward
evaluation (forward-only optimisations touch a minority of the runtime), and
`generate_reflections` re-derives a bit-identical list in six of seven stage
compiles — 12 % of the fit, compile-side, still unclaimed.

## WP-0601 — bounded LM: the expected tie, and the constraint that isn't one

Benchmarked **0.74–1.04× against scipy TRF**, reaching an identical minimum on
two of three protocols and ΔBIC −13 on the third. The tie is the *expected*
result — the normal-equation solve is a minority of runtime, so solver work is
Amdahl-bounded at ≈1.25× here, and Coelho's own published gains with a full A
matrix are R_ν 0.96–1.19. The driver's deliverable is **constraint vocabulary
scipy does not have**: the Stephens positivity cone σ²(M) = T·θ ≥ 0 is a
linear inequality on functionals of θ, and enforcing it takes round-robin
brucite from **12 of 43 reflections outside the cone to 0 of 43 — at a higher
Rwp** (the v0.5 method result again). Corundum never leaves the cone under
either driver.

Three claims this repo had recorded as measured were found wrong and corrected
in place: the cone guard tested σ² ≤ 0 (reporting the inert all-zero block as
unphysical, which manufactured the withdrawn "fires on isotropic and
anisotropic specimens alike" reading); and Coelho (2005)'s printed damping
factor is a no-op only until parameter removal shrinks N_k, after which it
degrades the step. Chasing an LM stall also found the **FCJ corner at
S/L = H/L** (identical Jacobian columns, ρ = +1.000, ~2 % analytic-column
error where every other column agrees to 1e-5, two drivers escaping in two
unprincipled directions) — written into the theory manual's method chapter as
the worked example of "the parameterisation, not the solver, stalls".

## WP-0602 — agent JSON surface: schema locked to the registries

One module: `agent.refine_json(dict) → dict` behind a strict three-task union
(refine / refine_multi / refine_sequential), a three-code error envelope
(`INVALID_REQUEST` with per-field dot-paths, `BACKEND_UNAVAILABLE`,
`REFINEMENT_FAILED`) sharing `Diagnostic`'s grammar, and `tool_definition()`
whose schema quotes backends/solvers/plans **from the live registries, with a
meta-test that fails when a registry member is missing** — the WP-0408 "fourth
name arrived two days after the third" lesson made executable. The 0308/0505
history asymmetries are answered by shape (joint fit → null history ids by
declaration; series → separate `series` arm with per-entry ids). Closed
inherited debts: `Provenance.solver` and `StageResult.n_constraint_truncations`
on every result, the `CONSTRAINT_ACTIVE` diagnostic (the only signal a declared
constraint was *active*), and the WP-0307 texture orphan claimed —
`refine_preferred_orientation` joined the Layer-2 vocabulary
(THRESHOLDS_VERSION 0.3).

## WP-0604 — theory manual: organised so it cannot drift

Ten chapters + front matter under `docs/manual/` (Sphinx + MyST +
sphinxcontrib-bibtex, furo): **59 numbered equations, each carrying a
`*Source:*` line naming the docstring it was transcribed from; 69 verified
bibliography entries; every convention warning beside its equation** (the
Stephens triple, size/strain letters, March-Dollase r by geometry, A vs
A* = 1/A, the two flat-plate "off" states, Sabine's sin²θ Bragg weight).

The anti-divergence design is executable rather than aspirational, which is
the acceptance:

- the build runs **`-W` inside the fast suite** (subprocess, ≈4 s) — an
  undefined substitution, missing citation or broken cross-reference fails
  the tests, not the reader;
- **ten fenced constants are injected from the live package** by
  `docs/manual/conf.py` as MyST substitutions — a retuned or renamed constant
  fails the build (the executable form of the WP-0305 Brindley-fence lesson);
- `tests/test_manual.py` asserts **every `*Source:*` symbol imports**, **every
  bib entry is cited** (both directions), and every labelled-equation page
  carries source lines — five guards, all in the fast suite.

Two docstring citations are deliberately absent from the bibliography because
their full records could not be verified against the papers and titles are
never fabricated: Andreev (1994), *J. Appl. Cryst.* 27, 288 and Deslattes &
Kessler (1985, Plenum). Recorded in the WP handover for whoever obtains the
PDFs.
