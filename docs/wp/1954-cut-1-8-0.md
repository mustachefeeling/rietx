## Goal

`docs/releases/1.8.0.md` names every user-facing change in `v1.7.0..main`, or
the handover says why one owes no note. `pyproject.version` reads `1.8.0`, the
three pre-upload nightly jobs are green on the release commit, and
`docs/milestones/v1.8.md` carries the ship block. What remains is
`RELEASING.md` steps 5-7: the tag, the `pypi` approval and the check from the
index.

## Context

The weekly cut under `RELEASING.md` § When: 1.7.0 was tagged 2026-10-08 on
`a9eaebb4`, and `main` has merged since. The staged notes and the v1.8 record
were opened that day and have been written into by each session that landed a
change.

**The trigger is WP-1929, not the date.** The maintainer asked on 2026-10-12
for the cut to follow 1929's merge (PR #909). At this WP's open 1929 held its
claim commit and no code. It changes what a user reads: `at_bound` and
`BOUND_HIT` from the driver's active set, a new floor diagnostic, marginal
esds beside a row on its bound, and `profile_interval`. So its notes, its new
code's skill row and every esd it moves arrive at its merge. Its acceptance
lists every acceptance-suite esd that moves by more than 1 %, and that list is
the cut's re-measure.

**The range at the open.** `v1.7.0..0f30d584`: 295 non-merge commits, 76
merges, 39 pull requests. Outside contributor pull requests under review at
the open: #871, #880, #888, #889, #902, #903, #904 and #675. The audit is run
on the tree at open and re-run over whatever merges before the version bump.

**Figures already re-measured on this range.** WP-1938 moved every converged
fit (physical units, LM the default). On 2026-10-11 it re-measured all 129
quoted rows of `VALIDATION.md` (`5aa667ff`, 17 moved by more than 1 %, each
listed in the staged notes) and ran a docs pass over Part 1, the skill, the
examples and the tutorials (`ad659455`). Nothing that moves a number merged
after it. 1546 re-measured about 70 stale figures by hand, and this cut should
not need to repeat that unless a merge after 1938 moves numbers.

**The Windows gate at the open.** The 2026-10-11 nightly (run 38136632756,
`12db370b`) failed one Windows test:
`test_joint_xray_neutron.py::test_the_structure_is_shared_and_the_instruments_are_not`,
with both histograms' W at 0.0. That tree predates 1938's merge. 1938's
`95fb4008` rewrote the assertion, because under softplus the two W values had
differed only by rounding. The next nightly on `main` is the check. The 3.12
and 3.13 Linux jobs were green on that run.

**The kernel wheel does not release with this cut.** rietx requires
`rietx-kernels` ≥ 1.1, and 1.2.0 was published 2026-10-11 (`RELEASING.md`
§ The kernel wheel).
