rule 6 wants Scope and Acceptance written *at the open*, and the v1.3 record
says plainly that rows written at ship are the weaker evidence, because a row
written after the fact cannot fail. Here the work landed first: v1.4.0 was
tagged 2026-09-14 16:13 and 496 commits have landed since under no milestone
at all. Every acceptance row this record carries is therefore an at-ship row,
and the record states that in place of pretending otherwise.
