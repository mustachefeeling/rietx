## Goal

`docs/releases/1.6.0.md` names every user-facing change in `v1.5.0..main`, or
the handover says why one owes no note. `pyproject.version` reads `1.6.0`, the
Windows nightly is green on the release commit, and `docs/milestones/v1.6.md`
carries the ship block. What remains is `RELEASING.md` steps 5-7: the tag, the
`pypi` approval and the check from the index.

## Context

WP-1540 changed the order: a release is numbered when it is cut and carries
whatever `main` holds, and a milestone that is not finished waits for the next
one. This is the first cut under that rule. It carries the staged 1.5.1 notes,
which since 1540 ship as 1.6.0, and everything that landed without a note.

**The body, measured from the tag range.** 1 173 non-merge commits in
`v1.5.0..origin/main` at `961f2eb5`: 73 WPs carry a prefix, 238 commits carry
none. Most unprefixed commits are an outside contributor's fixes landed under
issue numbers, and most of those change behaviour. The magnetic milestone's six
started WPs (1326-1329, 1343, 1418) landed most of their tasks from outside
and none is closed, so the magnetic work ships unfinished and the notes must
say what is missing.

**The public surface, diffed.** 13 new top-level exports, 302 new derived
surface names, none removed. `schema_version` 0.22 → 0.41,
`project_format_version` 1.3 → 1.4, `report_thresholds_version` 1.5 → 1.11,
`indexing_thresholds_version` 1.3 → 1.6. One capability flag renamed
(`secondary_extinction` → `primary_extinction`).

**The gate was red.** The Windows nightly, `RELEASING.md` step 4's
pre-upload gate, failed every night from 2026-09-28 on one test that handed
git a command line past Windows' 32 767 characters (WP-1506 inherited it), and
on 2026-10-02 on a second test asserting the sign of rounding noise.
